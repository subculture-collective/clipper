# clpr 30-day campaign plan

Draft experiment plan adapted from the Sputnik strategy. Day 1 begins when an owner selects a start date; no launch date or recurring automation is configured. Initial budget: organic distribution only; no paid placements proposed for execution. Suggested workload: 5–8 hours per week, to be adjusted after week one.

## Objective and first experience

Test whether viewers can find a relevant clip and understand how to keep or share it. Track useful product actions and feedback alongside reach. Choose one checked example search and one public playlist as demonstration material.

Before promotion, ask five willing testers to find a clip from a creator or topic they recognize. Record whether they succeed without help, approximate time, and where they get stuck. The imported handbook's 30–60-second framing is a usability prompt, not a performance guarantee.

## Channel choices

Start with two manageable channels: an owned social account for product demonstrations and one relevant community that permits the introduction. Reddit and Discord are research candidates, not assumed distribution access. Add a newsletter block if an existing opt-in list is available. Use a product walkthrough for Shorts or TikTok; redistribution of third-party clips requires its own rights review.

| Period | Work | Deliverable and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Days 1–7 | Check anonymous search/playback and authenticated playlist/share; run five first-use sessions; review community rules and destinations | Record failures; prepare one screenshot, one demo, one introduction, and one feedback question. Fix a broken core journey before promotion |
| Days 8–14 | Publish an approved demo and one permitted community introduction; answer feedback; invite up to three relevant people to test through an authorized outreach process | Record source links, replies, product actions, and confusing steps. No mass link drops |
| Days 15–21 | Turn the best-supported use case into a playlist showcase and a short walkthrough; audit search titles/descriptions for relevant existing pages | Use a real collection with a dated selection method. Do not label it “best on Twitch” or promise ranking coverage |
| Days 22–30 | Review acquisition and first-use results; publish a useful update or roundup if evidence supports it | Choose one channel to continue, one product obstacle to fix, and one message to retest |

No daily quota is required. A useful weekly demonstration with replies is a better workload assumption for this plan than publishing at a rate the owner cannot sustain.

## Asset checklist

- [ ] One actual search/result screenshot and a record of capture date and deployment.
- [ ] One complete demo following the [copy packet](packet.md), with captions and checked public playlist access.
- [ ] One square and one landscape artwork using Studio masters; include final alt text.
- [ ] Reviewed introduction, thread, newsletter block, and personalized outreach draft as needed.
- [ ] Checked links, current offer/account requirements, and a working feedback destination.
- [ ] Asset permissions and creator attribution recorded beside the originals.
- [ ] Named campaign owner and availability to answer replies and handle a broken destination.

## Measurement

These are proposed definitions, not claims about existing analytics. Confirm what the application measures before building a dashboard or reporting a result. Do not add tracking to the application as part of this document task.

| Question | Measure | Definition / limitation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Did the post reach people? | Impressions and link clicks | Platform-reported; compare within a channel and date window |
| Did visitors try the product? | Visits attributed to campaign | Use existing aggregate attribution if available; direct traffic has attribution limits |
| Did viewers find something? | Search-to-clip-open rate | Sessions with a search followed by a clip open / sessions with a search, in the same session; clip open is a proxy, not confirmed playback |
| Did people keep a collection? | Playlist-save completion rate | Completed saves / save attempts, using existing records if available; record account eligibility separately |
| Did a shared collection work? | Public playlist access checks | Manual success/failure in a separate session; not a retention metric |
| What should change? | Feedback themes and first-use observations | Count distinct respondents, describe the task, and keep permission for attributable quotes |

Use a weekly log with: period, owner, channel/post link, copy variant, destination, available measures, observed issue, and next decision. Record “unavailable” where instrumentation is missing. Do not report zeros for missing data or infer acquisition from views alone.

After week one, set a practical baseline and an experiment threshold from observed traffic. After two comparable posts on a channel, continue it if it brings useful actions or actionable feedback within the available time. Diagnose landing-page or first-use failures before increasing distribution. Small samples support a next experiment, not a claim of proven conversion lift.

## Launch-day and follow-up sequence

1. Recheck the selected product journey, destinations, final copy, and media.
2. Owner publishes the approved asset through the existing publishing workflow.
3. Answer questions and record failures; pause further distribution if the destination breaks.
4. Review feedback after 24–48 hours. Publish a follow-up only when there is a real fix, collection, or question worth sharing.
5. At day 30, keep the evidence and decide the next campaign. No automatic monthly regeneration is established by the imported notes.
