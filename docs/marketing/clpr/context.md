# clpr marketing context

Prepared October 19, 2026. Draft campaign direction based on repository scope and Studio branding. No new production journey or audience study was completed for this packet.

## Product and audience

**Public name:** clpr. **Destination:** [clpr.tv](https://clpr.tv/). **Maker:** [Subcult](https://subcult.tv/).

**One sentence:** clpr helps viewers find Twitch clips by creator, topic, and tag, save clips into playlists, and share a collection with people who missed the stream.

**Primary audience hypothesis:** viewers who remember a moment or creator but have lost the clip, and people who collect clips for friends. **Secondary hypothesis:** community curators who want a playlist around a creator, game, or topic. These are starting audiences to test, not measured segments. The imported age, viewing-hour, and creator-following ranges have no supporting study attached.

**Job to be done:** “Help me find that clip and send it to someone.”

**Positioning:** a viewer-facing place to discover and collect Twitch clips. Start with the remembered moment and show the search or playlist journey. Avoid claims about competitors unless a dated comparison supports them.

**Primary action:** Explore clpr → `https://clpr.tv/`. For a campaign about a specific collection, use a verified public playlist destination. “Explore creators” is a candidate CTA from the imported strategy; use it only with a checked creator-browsing destination.

## Message and voice

The current repository headline is **“Somebody clipped it. It's probably in here.”** Keep that as the default campaign line. The imported “Never miss the moment” can be tested as a hook, but it must not become a promise of complete coverage or immediate ingestion.

Write like a viewer describing a useful find. Short sentences, recognizable tasks, a little dry humor. Use “clip,” “creator,” “topic,” “tag,” and “playlist.” Explain a technical feature through what someone can do with it. Use lowercase `clpr` in public prose; preserve the supplied logo artwork.

- Use: “Remember the creator? Start there. Narrow the search by topic or tag.”
- Use: “Put the clips in a playlist and send one link.”
- Revise: “The ultimate AI-powered viral content engine.” There is no supported need for that claim.
- Revise: “Every Twitch moment, instantly.” Neither completeness nor latency is established.
