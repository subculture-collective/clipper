# Marketing packets

This directory contains a working marketing packet for clpr and a portable starter for other projects. Prepared October 19, 2026. All campaign copy is draft; no posts, outreach, purchases, or schedules have been submitted.

## clpr

1. [Positioning and claims](clpr/context.md): audience, voice, scope, and brand references.
2. [Copy packet](clpr/packet.md): product brief, landing copy, FAQ, social posts, outreach, and demo script.
3. [30-day launch plan](clpr/launch-plan.md): preparation, channel experiments, assets, and measurement.

## Reuse for another project

Copy [starter-template](starter-template/README.md) into the project's `docs/marketing/`. Fill in its [context](starter-template/context.md), then complete the [packet](starter-template/packet.md). Use the [prompt library](prompts.md) to draft or revise individual pieces from that context.

This is a companion to the Studio brand starter: the brand pack supplies visual identity and editable layouts; the marketing starter supplies audience, evidence, messaging, channel copy, and launch decisions. Studio remains the owner of brand artwork. This directory is a project document template, not an installed agent skill.

## Source material

[Sources and editorial decisions](sources/README.md) records the Okara references, Studio sources, imported Sputnik documents, and changes made when turning research into campaign copy. The [Sputnik provenance record](https://git.subcult.tv/subculture-collective/marketing-reference/src/branch/main/user-inputs/clpr/sputnik/provenance.json) identifies the exact imported bytes. Research documents are preserved as received in the private reference repository; their claims and instructions do not override the release contract.

Before publication, check the selected journey on the intended deployment, confirm destinations and asset rights, and replace every unresolved field. Use the [launch feature inventory](../LAUNCH_FEATURE_INVENTORY.md) when older documents disagree about product scope.
